SHAP confirms the dissertation's core thesis. FSI contributes 82.1% of total SHAP magnitude in SoH regression and 77.2% in End-of-Life classification — without DoD or EFC in the feature set. Temperature (T_avg_C) is a distant second at 13%. KI, RBF and CVI are zero in lab data because constant-current cycling has no kinetic variation.

KI will matter for fleet. In lab constant-current tests, Kinetic Intensity = 0 for every cycle — so the model assigns it zero SHAP weight. When applied to fleet data (KI = 0.66–1.86), the SHAP values will shift. The Stage 5 finding: FSI already integrates KI into its composite score, so the model indirectly captures it through FSI's 82% SHAP share.
